Contact the team

Contact your team and coach as soon as possible to confirm the first training night, game day, venue, and start time. Do not leave the first message until the last minute.

Find fixture and game details

Fixtures are available through PlayHQ and the Fixtures and Season Dates area of the club website.

To find game details:

  1. Select the relevant competition, season, and age group.
  2. Select your Kangas team.
  3. Check the fixture the day before each game because late venue and time changes can occur.
  4. Notify your team of the game details.

Kangas teams are usually listed as NK followed by the team number, such as NK14.1.

Organise training supervision

A responsible adult must be present at every training session. Please roster a parent on duty for each training night.

The duty parent should:

  • Make sure the hall is occupied only by club members involved in training.
  • Position themselves where they can see the stadium entrance.
  • Keep children off other venue equipment.
  • Make sure chairs are returned after use and not dragged across the floor.
  • Make sure there is no smoking in the stadium or toilets.

Training venue security

When training finishes, the duty parent should wait until the next team arrives before leaving. If the next team does not arrive within about 15 minutes, turn out the lights, close the door, and advise the venue contact through the relevant WhatsApp group or contact Lyn Moran on 0429 166 411 so the hall can be locked.

Training should only be cancelled in exceptional circumstances. The venue coordinator needs at least a full week’s notice where possible so training times can be reallocated. Always advise the venue coordinator if your team is not training.

During finals, if your team does not make finals, notify Lyn immediately so the training slot can be reallocated and the hall is not left unattended.

Electronic scoring

The Team Manager must check active players in the scoring system at the beginning of the game and adjust details as required, including singlet numbers.

If a player is new or filling in, add their full details into the scoring system. This includes full name, date of birth, address, and singlet number.

If a player’s singlet number is incorrect in PlayHQ, it can be manually changed on the iPad before the game. To permanently update a player’s singlet number, email the Registrar at Registrar@nunawadingkangas.com.au.

Arrange a scorer

Arrange a scorer for every game and encourage parents to share the responsibility. A season roster works well.

If there is a dispute about the score, do not allow the game to be closed off on the scoring device before calling the venue supervisor to sort it out on the spot. The scoring device is the official game record.

Team difficulties

If a player leaves the team, a coach repeatedly misses training, or you have an issue that cannot be resolved within the team, contact your Age Group Coordinator. The Club can only help when it knows there is an issue.

Communication

Distribute fixture information and club notices to the team and coach. Club information will usually be sent to Team Managers by email for forwarding to families.

Routinely check for fixture changes, ladder updates, rule changes, and reasons for lost points. Any dispute over ladder positions should be referred to the coordinator.

Player substitution and walkovers

Four players are needed to start a game. If a team cannot field four players, the team will cause a walkover and incur a $150 Association fine.

If you may be short of players:

  • Ask players to notify you early if unavailable.
  • Contact your Age Group Coordinator for help with eligible fill-in players.
  • Check the Teammo and Walkovers guidance before lodging a walkover or forfeit.
  • Never allow a player to play under another player’s name. Basketball Victoria penalties can be severe and may apply to players, coaches, and officials.

Concussion and injuries

Nunawading Kangas follows Basketball Australia concussion guidance to protect player health and wellbeing.

If a player receives a significant injury during a game, ask the referee to note the injury on the scoring system or scoresheet. This record may be needed for an insurance claim.

If a player is injured before a game or training session, the player or family must tell the coach so the risk of further injury can be managed.

Finals qualification

Each season, the Association confirms the minimum number of games players must play to qualify for finals. Games as a fill-in do not count towards finals qualification for the team they filled in for.

If a player is injured and misses several games, they may need a medical certificate. Pass this to the Age Group Coordinator before the final rounds of the season.
