## When can we use fill-in players?

If your team has five or fewer available players, you can use fill-in players to bring the team up to six players.

Penalties may apply if teams do not follow the fill-in player rules.

## How many games can a fill-in player play?

Any player who fills in for three games must then register with the Club before playing further games for your team.

Team Managers should notify the Registrar once a player has filled in for three games if the team intends to use that player again.

Penalties can apply when a player fills in more than three games while unregistered. This may include finals ineligibility or forfeiting games in which the player participated.

To register a player, email [Registrar@nunawadingkangas.com.au](mailto:Registrar@nunawadingkangas.com.au).

## Who can fill in?

Fill-in players may only be sourced:

- From within Nunawading Kangas and in accordance with the prescribed fill-in matrix.
- From a lower age group and usually a lower grade.
- From players who are of the correct age and do not hold a current Basketball Victoria licence with another junior basketball competition.

## Same club requirement

A Club can source a fill-in player only from within its own Club. The player may come from a team on the same day of competition or from another MEBA competition day or night, provided they meet the age and grade requirements.

Examples:

- A Saturday boys player can fill in for a Friday boys team from the same club if they do not play for another club on Saturday and meet the age and grade criteria.
- An Under 13 girls player playing on Saturday cannot fill in for a Monday Under 13 team, but an eligible Under 11 player may meet the age criteria.

## Check before using a fill-in

If you require a fill-in player, check with your Age Group Coordinator before the game to make sure the player qualifies for your grade.

Using an ineligible player can cause your team to forfeit points even if it wins.

Never allow a player to play under another player's name.
