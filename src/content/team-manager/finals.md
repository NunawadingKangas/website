## Finals eligibility

Each season, the Association confirms how many games each player must play to qualify for finals. Team Managers should check their team list in PlayHQ to confirm every player has played enough games.

Byes and games won because of opposition forfeits may be included in the games played count, depending on Association rules for that season.

If a player misses several games due to injury or illness, they may be able to provide a medical certificate and request an exemption. Submit these requests to the Registrar for submission to the Association.

## Fill-in players for finals

If your team has fewer than six players qualified for finals, you may apply for exemptions for enough fill-in players to give the team six players, but no more than that.

Contact the Registrar to apply for finals fill-in exemptions.

As a general rule:

- Fill-in players should come from a lower age group and lower grade.
- If your team used fill-in players during the season, the Association is likely to expect finals exemption requests to be for those players.
- Players who filled in the most during the season are more likely to be preferred.
- Approved players will be added to the scoring program by the Association before the game.

## Finals fixtures

Finals fixtures are usually shown only after the fixture is updated with the results of the last normal round.

Check the fixture carefully and keep families updated.

## Split grades

If your team played in a split grade during the season, such as `A/B1` or `D/D2`, the grade may be split for finals.

For example, in an eight-team `A/B1` grade:

| Season grade | Split for finals | Elimination Finals | Grand Finals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A/B1 | Top 4 teams are in A grade | Two elimination finals | Winners play in the A grade Grand Final |
| A/B1 | Bottom 4 teams are in B1 grade | Two elimination finals | Winners play in the B1 Grand Final |

In a six-team `D/D2` grade:

| Season grade | Split for finals | Elimination Finals | Grand Finals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| D/D2 | Top 2 teams are in D grade | No elimination finals | Top 2 teams go straight to the D grade Grand Final |
| D/D2 | Bottom 4 teams are in D2 grade | Two elimination finals | Winners play in the D2 Grand Final |

## Team sheet fees

Elimination finals team sheet fees are paid by teams, so the Team Manager should collect from families if required.

Grand Final team sheet fees are paid by the Club for all teams.

## Scorers for finals

Your team must provide a scorer for finals games. For Grand Finals, the Association will usually provide a third scorer to sit on the bench and check that each club scorer marks the score correctly.

## Under 8 teams

Under 8 teams do not play elimination or Grand Finals. They usually play additional normal rounds during the finals period. At the end of the last game, players are usually presented with a small Association medal for participating in the season.
