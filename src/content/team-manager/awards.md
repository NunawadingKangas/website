## Club awards

At the end of each season, two team awards are usually presented at Presentation Day:

- Most Valuable Player
- Coaches Award

The Club will request award selections toward the end of the season so trophies can be prepared in advance.

## Most Valuable Player

The MVP recognises a player who has made a strong contribution to the team's basketball performance.

Criteria may include:

- Great defence
- Confident offensive skills
- Strong team play
- Fundamental ball skills
- Adaptability
- Competitiveness
- Enthusiasm

## Coaches Award

The Coaches Award recognises outstanding contribution, effort, improvement, and positive team behaviours.

Criteria may include:

- Most improved player
- Strong effort at training or games
- Reliable attendance at games and training
- Listening to the coach
- Following coach instructions
- Supporting teammates
- Trying hard every time they step on court

## Age group notes

All Under 8 players receive a trophy at the end of the season for participation.

Under 19 players do not receive trophies.

## Season medals

The Club recognises 5, 10, 15, 20, and 30 seasons played at Nunawading Kangas. Players who reach these milestones receive a medal acknowledging their contribution to the Club.

## Lifetime awards

Lifetime Awards recognise members who have given significant time and effort to Nunawading Kangas over a number of years through playing, coaching, committee service, or other club contribution.

For questions, contact the Trophy Coordinator at [trophies@nunawadingkangas.com.au](mailto:trophies@nunawadingkangas.com.au).
