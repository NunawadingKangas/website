## Singlet number clashes

The Club works hard to avoid new players ordering a singlet number already worn by an existing Kangas player, but clashes can still happen.

If two players in your team have the same singlet number, the player who has been with Nunawading Kangas the longest keeps wearing their singlet. The Club will loan the newer player an alternate singlet for the season.

Contact the Uniform Coordinator at [Uniforms@nunawadingkangas.com.au](mailto:Uniforms@nunawadingkangas.com.au) if you need help with a number clash.

## Colour clashes

Kangas has yellow colour priority over other teams in the competition. Where a colour clash occurs with another club, the opposition should change singlets.

## Playing another Kangas team

When two Kangas teams play each other, the team listed first on the fixture or scoresheet must wear alternate tops. Most players should have reversible singlets and can wear the black side.

If anyone does not have a reversible singlet, contact the Uniform Coordinator before the game.

You can also hire a set of alternate tops from the playing venue on the day. Ask at the ticket box. A hire fee usually applies, with part of the amount refunded when the tops are returned after the match.

## Plan ahead

Check your fixture in advance for Kangas-versus-Kangas games and potential clashes. Do not wait until game day to organise alternate tops.
