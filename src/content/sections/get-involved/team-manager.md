Thanks for taking on this valuable role for the season. Team Managers are an essential link between the Club, coaches, players, and families.

Please register as a Team Manager in PlayHQ so the Club can add your details to the communication list and make sure your team receives important season updates. If the registration form is not open, email the Registrar at [Registrar@nunawadingkangas.com.au](mailto:Registrar@nunawadingkangas.com.au).

## Getting started

Before the season begins:

- Confirm the first game details and training time.
- Download or request your team list with player and family contact details.
- Contact players and families with game and training details.
- Set up a team communication channel, such as WhatsApp.
- Check singlet numbers for clashes and contact the Uniform Coordinator if needed.
- Organise a scoring roster for the season.

On game day:

- Check active players in the electronic scoring system.
- Update player details or singlet numbers if required.
- Make sure you have enough players before the game.
- Take a photo of the final scoring screen in case results do not upload correctly.
